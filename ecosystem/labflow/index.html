LabFlow — a released Zaions product

LabFlow joins patients, orders, samples and results in one laboratory system. Its meaningful boundary is the validation step between a draft result and a released one.

The question: When is a lab result ready to leave the workflow?

What is kept together?​

Patient and order records sit alongside samples and results. Billing, inventory and quality control are part of the published product too, so the result does not live in an isolated form.

What happens before release?​

The roster names a validation workflow from draft to released. That is the step I would inspect when assessing whether the software respects the way a laboratory works.

What does this page claim?​

It describes the product workflow, not clinical accuracy or a regulatory certification. Those would need their own evidence and are not supplied by a portfolio link.

Where is the consequential handoff?​

Between a draft result and a released one. LabFlow keeps patient records, orders, samples and results in one system, but keeping them together is only the starting point. The validation workflow is the point at which a result changes status and becomes ready for its next reader. Billing, inventory and quality control are part of the same published product, so I would also inspect how those records sit beside an order. The release record establishes that the steps exist. It does not establish clinical accuracy, laboratory accreditation or the rules a particular jurisdiction requires; those claims need evidence beyond a portfolio page.

From the release record​

  • Run your diagnostic lab end to end.
  • Patients, orders, samples and results in one system
  • Validation workflow from draft to released
  • Billing, inventory and quality control built in
