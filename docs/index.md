I am Ahsan Mahmood, an AI-native full-stack developer in Lahore with 8+ years of hands-on experience. AI agents write most of my code now; I decide what they build, review it and confirm it works. That is one way to get software made. These are the other five, including the jobs where I would send you to them.

## How I work now

I can write all of this by hand, and for most of my career I did. I work through AI agents now because they produce code far faster than I can. I decide what gets built and how, I tell them, and I review what comes back. Nothing is finished until I have confirmed it is correct.

What took me a month now takes a day or a week, and I would put the change at 10 to 100 times. That is my own statement from doing the work rather than a measurement, so read it beside the dates here.

## Five other ways to get this built, and how I compare

None of these is the wrong answer. Each one fits some jobs better than I do, and its page says which.

- [An agency](/compare/agency) — Choose it when the work needs several people at the same hour.
- [An inexperienced developer](/compare/inexperienced-developer) — Choose it when the job is small and the mistakes are cheap.
- [A vibe coder](/compare/vibe-coder) — Choose it when you want to test an idea on real people this week.
- [A freelancer from a large marketplace](/compare/freelancer-marketplace) — Choose it for a small job with a clear finish.
- [Doing it in-house](/compare/in-house) — Choose it when the software is the business and needs someone every day for years.
