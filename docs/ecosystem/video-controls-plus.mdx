Video Controls Plus adds detailed controls to HTML5 video: a broad speed range, an A–B loop, frame stepping, audio boost, filters, screenshots and timestamped notes.

**The question:** What can a viewer do beyond pressing play?

## How precise can playback be?

The published speed range is 0.1× to 16×. A–B looping and frame stepping give a viewer ways to repeat or inspect a small part of a video.

## What can be kept from a moment?

Screenshots and timestamped notes attach something useful to a point in playback. The roster also records audio boost up to 400% and colour filters.

## How is it operated?

More than 40 shortcuts are listed. The released extension site is the place to inspect the current controls and their settings.
