Tilawah offers recorded Tajweed and Tafseer lessons with progress tracking. A learner can study at their own pace and book live one-on-one recitation sessions in their timezone.

**The question:** How does a learner return to a recitation lesson?

## What is available without a booking?

The recorded lessons and progress record support self-paced study. The roster does not claim a particular curriculum length or learning outcome.

## When does a teacher join?

Live one-on-one recitation sessions can be booked in the learner’s timezone. That is a separate path from opening a recorded lesson.

## What can a visitor open?

The live site shows current lessons and booking.
