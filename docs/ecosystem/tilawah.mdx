Tilawah offers recorded Tajweed and Tafseer lessons with progress tracking. A learner can study at their own pace and book live one-on-one recitation sessions in their timezone.

**The question:** How does a learner return to a recitation lesson?

## What is available without a booking?

The recorded lessons and progress record support self-paced study. The roster does not claim a particular curriculum length or learning outcome.

## When does a teacher join?

Live one-on-one recitation sessions can be booked in the learner’s timezone. That is a separate path from opening a recorded lesson.

## What can a visitor open?

The live site shows current lessons and booking.

## What can a learner choose next?

A recorded Tajweed or Tafseer lesson can be taken at the learner's pace, with progress kept across the lessons. A live one-on-one recitation session is a separate choice, booked in the learner's timezone. I would inspect those paths independently: one starts with opening a lesson, the other with choosing a time. The roster does not state a curriculum length or guarantee a learning outcome, so this page should not add either. The live product shows its current lessons and booking flow. A useful account of it leaves room for a learner to decide whether recorded study or a session fits the next step.

The difference between opening a recording and reserving a person’s time should remain obvious at the first decision.
