Trizlink combines branded short links, link-in-bio pages and click analytics. Custom slugs and domains make the destination recognisable before a visitor follows it.

**The question:** What happens after a short link is shared?

## What does a creator publish?

A creator can make a short link with a custom slug or a link-in-bio page. Custom domains are part of the published product.

## What can they measure?

The roster records click analytics. I would inspect the product’s current reporting before claiming a specific metric or attribution method.

## Where do the utilities fit?

More than 50 built-in utilities are listed, but the core link workflow remains the reason to open Trizlink. A utility count does not describe how a link resolves.
