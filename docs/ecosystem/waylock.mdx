Waylock sits over Google Maps for drivers and couriers. A driver can hold chosen stops in place, order the rest and keep the chosen route visible after a wrong turn.

**The question:** What happens to a planned route after a wrong turn?

## What is fixed before departure?

Chosen stops can stay in place while other stops are ordered around them. That lets a driver protect a commitment without freezing every part of the day.

## What appears after a detour?

The chosen route remains visible beside an option to return or take a new route. A wrong turn does not silently erase the plan the driver made.

## Where is the platform boundary?

It is a planning layer over Google Maps, not a replacement for it.

## What survives a wrong turn?

The chosen route remains visible. Waylock lets a driver or courier hold selected stops in place while ordering the rest around them, then presents a choice to return to the old route or take a new one after a detour. I would inspect how those fixed stops are shown before departure and how the alternative appears when the road changes. The product is a planning layer over Google Maps, which is an important limit: it does not replace the underlying map service. Its value is in preserving a driver's decision while still allowing a new decision when the route changes.

## From the release record

- Waylock is an Android planning layer over Google Maps for drivers and couriers.
- A driver can hold chosen stops in place and order the rest around them.
- After a wrong turn, the chosen route stays visible beside the option to return or take a new route.
