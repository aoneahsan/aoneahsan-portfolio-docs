Eat This starts with the way a person is choosing food: a nutrient, a feeling or a time of day. The result is grounded in a serving rather than an unexplained recommendation.

**The question:** What does one serving actually provide?

## How does the search begin?

Someone can search by nutrient, feeling or time of day. That makes three distinct ways into the same food choice, without requiring the person to know a food name first.

## What does the result quantify?

The app shows how much of a nutrient one serving gives and identifies a cheap pick. A serving is the unit that lets a person compare a result with what they might actually eat.

## Where does the app stop?

Food search flags when a question belongs with a doctor; it does not give medical advice.
