I am Ahsan Mahmood, a full-stack developer in Lahore. This guide sits beside [my portfolio](https://aoneahsan.com/): the projects I can show, the work I take on, and the released products I have built with Zaions. I have more than eight years of hands-on development experience. I can write the code myself. Today I also direct AI agents, read what they produce and check the result against the product people will actually use.

The difference matters at both ends of a build. Choosing what to make, setting boundaries and deciding whether the finished work is correct still take experience. An agent can move quickly through an implementation; it cannot take responsibility for the decision to ship it. That remains mine.

## Why this portfolio exists

I want the working product to carry more weight than a claim about it. A client project I can show belongs here. So do my skills and the products that are live. Each release in [the ecosystem](/ecosystem/) has a direct destination; you can leave this guide and inspect it yourself. A private or unfinished project does not join that list because a polished description would tell you less than a working page.

The comparison pages in this guide describe ways of getting software built. They do not compare other people's profiles. They explain the decisions a buyer will face when choosing an agency, a marketplace, an early-career developer or one person who can carry the work from scope to release. The answer depends on the job. The dates, prices and limits on the main site are there so you can decide with more than a slogan.

## This portfolio's journey

These dates describe this site, not a delivery promise for somebody else's project.

- **31 July 2026:** Planning and the first design pages began.
- **16 August 2026:** The rebuilt app received its first commit.
- **21 August 2026:** The rebuild took over aoneahsan.com.
- **5 November 2026:** The site moved to a new host.

The design pages came before the rebuilt app. The live site then gave the work a harder test: pages had to render, links had to arrive at the right place, and the build had to keep serving after I stopped looking at it. The guide you are reading is part of the same record. It explains the work without requiring someone to sign in first.

## How I work

I decide what gets built. Then I check it. Agents help me move through implementation, but the code, browser behaviour and handover still need review. A green build is one piece of evidence; it does not tell me whether a person can finish the task on a phone, whether a link leads to a live release, or whether an answer on a public page is true.

If you have a project in mind, [contact me through the portfolio](https://aoneahsan.com/contact). Tell me what it needs to do. I will look at the work and say where I fit. You can also [browse the released Zaions products](/ecosystem/) or [visit Zaions](https://zaions.com) to see the team around them.
